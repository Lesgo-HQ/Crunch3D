# Code of conduct

Crunch3D welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed spaces.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate, explain technical disagreements, and focus feedback on the work. Respect privacy, credit other people's contributions, and accept reasonable requests to stop disruptive behavior.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, impersonation, and repeated personal attacks are not acceptable. Do not publish another person's private information without consent.

## Reporting

Contact a member of the [maintainer team](MAINTAINERS.md) through a private contact method they publicly provide on their GitHub profile. If none is available, ask for a private contact route in a repository issue without including names, allegations, or sensitive details. Do not post an incident report publicly. If a report concerns a maintainer, contact a different maintainer.

## Enforcement

Maintainers review reports in context and share details only as needed to investigate and respond. A maintainer involved in a report should not decide its outcome. Responses may include a request to change behavior, a warning, removal of content, or a temporary or permanent restriction from project spaces, depending on severity and recurrence.

A person affected by a moderation decision may ask an uninvolved maintainer to reconsider it through the same reporting route. Maintainers are expected to follow this policy too.
